FRAMEFORGE 0.2.6

Capture: press Ctrl plus the tilde/backtick key (no Shift needed).
Completed screenshots open in the editor and copy automatically to the clipboard.
Paste with Ctrl+V. After adding annotations, use Copy image for the edited image.

SYSTEM TRAY
Closing the editor keeps FrameForge running in the system tray by default.
Double-click the purple F icon to reopen the editor.
The icon may be inside Windows' hidden-icons overflow near the clock.
Right-click it for capture, shortcuts, preferences, and Exit FrameForge.
Exit FrameForge quits completely and releases the capture shortcuts.

PREFERENCES
Turn Start FrameForge when I sign in to Windows on to launch quietly in the tray.
Turn Keep running in the tray off if you prefer the window's Close button to exit.
Keyboard shortcuts can be changed independently in Keyboard shortcuts.

RECENT CAPTURES
Use the x button on a recent capture, or right-click it and choose Delete capture.
The editable project and its PNG are moved together into Deleted Captures.
Undo delete restores them. Deleting the open capture clears the editor.
Right-click Open capture folder and choose Open deleted captures to recover
older deleted files or remove them permanently in File Explorer.
Deleted files remain on disk until you remove them; exports elsewhere are untouched.

YOUR DATA
Captures and settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FrameForge.
Uninstalling the app preserves this data.
No account, cloud upload, or network service is used.

VIDEO SUPPORT
Recording, trim, and GIF export use a separately installed FFmpeg executable.
The installer does not include FFmpeg. It already exists on the development PC.
On another PC, obtain FFmpeg from https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and put
ffmpeg.exe in a tools folder beside FrameForge.exe, or make it available in PATH.
Restart FrameForge after installing it. Screenshots, editing, and OCR work without it.
